# Market OS — A Market Layer for Safe Distribution of Programmable Code on Top of the Outer OS

## 0. Purpose of Market OS

Market OS is an economic layer designed to safely distribute programmable code on top of the Outer OS.  
In traditional programming environments, code distribution has only two states: **shared** or **stolen**.  
There has been no structural mechanism that allows code to be safely circulated.

Market OS solves this by providing a structural market where:

- Code can be distributed in a **safe and usable form**  
- Copyright is **structurally protected**  
- All languages can be safely combined without dependency explosions  
- Companies can verify behavior **before purchasing**  
- AI can combine code safely without causing system failures  
- Authors retain ownership while allowing controlled distribution  

Market OS functions as the **market layer of the Structure OS**,  
built on top of the Outer OS and the 10-Action grammar.

---

## 1. Preconditions: Safety of Combination via PMP

Market OS is enabled by the structural guarantees of PMP:

### ● Approval Flow  
All logic must be approved before combination, preventing uncontrolled behavior.

### ● Black Box (core.php)  
Internal logic is always protected and cannot be stolen.

### ● Outer OS (public/server separation)  
Combination is executed safely outside the internal logic boundary.

---

## Why Combination Explosions Do Not Occur (PMP Structure)

In traditional programming, combining multiple pieces of code often causes:

- Dependency conflicts  
- Internal logic collisions  
- Language-level incompatibilities  
- Exponential failure cascades  

This is known as **combination explosion**.

PMP prevents this through:

### 1. Combination via “10 Actions” instead of languages  
All languages are converted into a shared grammar:  
**query / filter / sort / render / save / update / delete / route / notify / authenticate**

### 2. Internal logic is black-boxed  
Parts do not see each other’s internals.  
`model.php` acts as a safe interface.

### 3. Combination runs in the Outer OS  
Internal logic is never directly affected.

### 4. Approval flow prevents unsafe logic  
AI-generated or complex logic cannot combine without approval.

---

## 2. Ownership of Parts

Ownership always remains with the author.

- Authors keep the **original source**  
- The market stores **approved replicas**  
- Originals and replicas are structurally separated  
- Copyright always stays with the author  
- The market distributes replicas as products  

---

## 3. Copyright Separation

Thanks to PMP’s black-box structure:

- **Original (author’s work)**  
  → Controlled by the author, including view rights.

- **Replica (approved market copy)**  
  → Used for combination, preview, and distribution.

---

## 4. View Rights

View rights are granted only when the author allows:

- Viewing internal logic  
- Extending or modifying the logic  
- Commercial usage decisions  

---

## 5. Combine Rights

Combine rights allow users to test parts through the **Combine Preview UI**:

- Internal logic remains protected  
- Only results are shown  
- Theft is structurally impossible  
- AI combination is safe  

---

## 6. Combine Preview (Combination UI)

Combine Preview is the core feature of Market OS.  
It enables something previously impossible:

> **Testing combinations of code without exposing internal logic.**

### Why Combine Preview Was Impossible Before

Traditional programming exposes internal logic during combination:

- Copyright cannot be protected  
- Code is stolen upon combination  
- Languages cannot be safely mixed  
- AI may combine code dangerously  
- Combination explosions occur  

Thus:

> **“Combine and test” was fundamentally incompatible with copyright.**

### Why Combine Preview Works in Market OS

- Combination runs in the Outer OS  
- Internal logic is black-boxed  
- Only results are shown  
- Combination uses 10 Actions, not languages  
- Approval flow ensures safety  

### Market Impact

Combine Preview enables:

- Pre-purchase behavior verification  
- Safe enterprise adoption  
- Safe AI-driven combination  
- Cross-language combination  
- Market value formed by combination logs  
- Structural prevention of code theft

## 8. Minimal Trade Model (Parts Market Model)

Market OS uses a **minimal trade model** designed for structural safety.

### ● Pricing is determined by “rights”

- Combine rights  
- View rights  
- Commercial rights  

This avoids complex pricing competition and ensures fairness.

### ● The market sells “replicas”

- Authors keep originals  
- Market stores approved replicas  
- Only replicas are sold  
- Originals remain protected  

### ● Flow: Combine Preview → Purchase

1. User tests behavior via Combine Preview  
2. Internal logic stays protected  
3. Only results are shown  
4. User purchases the replica if satisfied

Market OS becomes a **self-optimizing market** over time.

Manages ownership, replicas, copyright separation,  
Combine Preview, trade, and rights.
